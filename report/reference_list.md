# Final Reference List (reviewed)

Three research agents found about 35 candidate sources, one agent per research question. A fourth reviewer agent re-checked every source independently against the red-flag checklist:
- predatory journals and missing DOIs
- conflicts of interest and preprints
- small samples
- correlation treated as causation
- over-generalised claims

These are the **final 8**, the maximum the task sheet allows. All 8 are peer-reviewed and have a working DOI.

   - whether to add "(Accessed: date)" after DOIs

4. Take screenshots of each source for **Appendix C**: title, first author and journal.
